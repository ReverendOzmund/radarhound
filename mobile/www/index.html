Radarhound

Radar, satellite, lightning and storm tools.

Everything runs on this phone, straight from BoM, Open-Meteo, NASA and LightningMaps. It uses more data and battery while open. Server-only: 48-hour history, raw volume scans, raw satellite bands and the verification log. With the app closed, alerts cover BoM warnings and lightning nearby.

In quiet hours lightning, storm-approaching and rain alerts are silent. Warnings still sound.

At home: use the server's address on your Wi-Fi, for example http://192.168.1.50:8686.

Away from home: install Tailscale on the server and this phone, then use the server's Tailscale address, for example https://myserver.tail1234.ts.net. The https address also unlocks the "Me" location button.

To switch servers later, tap ⇄ at the top of Radarhound.
